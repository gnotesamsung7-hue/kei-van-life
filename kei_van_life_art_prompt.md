# Kei Van Life: art prompts

The same prompts are inside the game under Extras > Art prompts, where each one has a button to copy the prompt and a button to copy its file name. The game draws everything in 3D by itself, so this art is optional.

Paint colours from the 2026 DA17V chart: Pearl White #f3f1ea, White #ffffff, Moss Gray Metallic #8f9086, Tool Orange #e39a3b, Silky Silver Metallic #c3c9d1, Ivy Green Metallic #9aa287, Bluish Black Pearl #16242b, Denim Blue Metallic #2f6377.
